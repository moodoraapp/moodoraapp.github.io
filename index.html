FAQ

Good to know

Is Moodora free?

Yes, it is free to download and use. Premium unlocks your full history, deeper insights, a weekly coaching session and color themes. New subscribers can try it free for 7 days.

Do I need an account?

No. Just type your name; we never ask for an email or a password.

Is Moo a therapist?

No. Moo is a supportive companion, not therapy or a medical service. Moodora does not replace professional care.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Moodora is iPhone only for now.

Which languages?

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Japanese and Korean.
