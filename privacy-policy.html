Introduction

Moodora ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Data You Provide (kept on your device)

  • Mood Entries: Emotions, notes, timestamps, and photos you choose to record
  • Activity Data: Activities and tags you associate with your moods
  • Routines and Goals: Your own routines, goals and check marks
  • App Preferences: Theme settings, notification preferences, and language choice
  • Achievement Progress: Streak data and unlocked achievements

If You Use Streak Buddy (kept on our server)

  • The name you enter and your invite code
  • Your streak and whether you logged today
  • Motivation messages you send to your buddy
  • Shared routines, including any short text you write for a step, and your daily check marks
  • Reports you send and buddies you block

Collected Automatically

  • A random anonymous user ID (you never create an account)
  • Your Premium status
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which are not linked to your user ID
  • App install and launch events with technical device data (device model, OS and app version), sent to Meta to measure our ads; not linked to your user ID

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your email address or phone number (unless you email us)
  • Location data
  • Your contacts
  • Browsing history
  • Data from other apps

Data Storage

Your mood entries, notes and photos are stored on your device. We do not upload them to our servers, except for the text sent for an AI reply when you use AI features (see below).

Our server runs on Google Firebase: the database is in the European Union (Belgium) and the server functions run in the United States. It keeps your anonymous user ID, your Premium status and, if you use Streak Buddy, the Streak Buddy data listed above. Shared routines are deleted when they are ended, when the pairing is removed or when a buddy is blocked. Daily AI insights are cached for 24 hours; expired copies are deleted automatically.

Optional Cloud Backup

If you choose to use iCloud backup:

  • Data is stored in your personal iCloud account
  • We do not have access to your cloud backup
  • Backup is encrypted by Apple

AI Features

AI features (AI Coach, insights and weekly analysis) work only after you allow them, and you can turn them off anytime in Settings › Privacy. When they are on:

  • Your message and a summary of your recent entries (moods, emotions and notes) are sent through our server to OpenAI to create a reply
  • Data is transmitted securely (HTTPS)
  • OpenAI does not use this data to train models and may keep it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring
  • We do not store your AI conversations on our server

Streak Buddy

Streak Buddy is optional. When you pair with a friend, your buddy sees your name, your streak, whether you logged today and your progress in shared routines, including the steps you added.

  • Report: If a shared routine contains inappropriate text, you can report it. The routine, its text, both users' anonymous IDs and your buddy's name are sent to us for review. We may remove content or end the pairing.
  • Block: Blocking ends the pairing, deletes your shared routines and prevents you from being paired again. We keep a record of the block.
  • Text in shared routines is checked automatically: profanity, links, email addresses and phone numbers are not accepted.

Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers to run the app:

Google Firebase

  • Anonymous sign-in, our server functions and database (Streak Buddy, Premium status)
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Analytics) and crash reports (Crashlytics)

OpenAI

  • Creates AI replies, only when you have allowed AI features

RevenueCat (Subscriptions)

  • Handles subscription purchases with your anonymous user ID
  • Does not receive your mood data

Meta (Facebook) SDK — ad measurement

  • Tells Meta when the app is installed and opened, together with technical data such as device model, OS and app version, so we can measure how many installs our ads on Facebook and Instagram bring (through Apple's SKAdNetwork and Meta's Aggregated Event Measurement)
  • Does not receive your mood entries, notes, photos, AI chats or Streak Buddy data
  • We do not ask for tracking permission and do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • See Meta's Privacy Policy

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell or trade your personal data. We share data only as described above: with OpenAI when you use AI features, with your buddy when you use Streak Buddy, with Meta to measure our ads (install and launch events only), and with the service providers that run the app. Otherwise your data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it yourself.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Export: Download your data anytime (CSV or JSON format)
  • Delete: Settings › Delete account removes all data on your device and your iCloud backup
  • Server data: Email us to access or delete your Streak Buddy data on our server; removing your buddy ends sharing and deletes your shared routines
  • AI: Turn AI features off anytime in Settings › Privacy
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all data on your device

Children's Privacy

Moodora is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures:

  • Data on your device is protected by your device's security
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all network requests
  • Server data can only be reached through authenticated requests

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
