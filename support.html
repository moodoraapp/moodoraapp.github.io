Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my mood?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen, select your current mood emoji, optionally add a note or photo, and save. It takes just seconds!

What is a streak?

A streak counts consecutive days you've logged your mood. Building streaks helps you develop a consistent self-reflection habit and unlocks achievements.

How does the AI Coach work?

The AI Coach analyzes your mood patterns over time and provides personalized insights and suggestions. After 7 days of logging, you'll start seeing meaningful patterns.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your mood entries, notes and photos are stored on your device. AI features work only after you allow them; then your message and a summary of your recent entries are sent through our server to OpenAI to create a reply, and they are not used to train models. If you use Streak Buddy, your name, streak, messages and shared routines are kept on our server. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account. On your iPhone, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions and choose Moodora to manage or cancel it.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the Today screen, tap the profile icon, open Go Premium and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. This restores any active subscription linked to your Apple Account.

Can I export my data?

Yes! Go to Settings > Data Management > Export as CSV to download all your mood entries.
